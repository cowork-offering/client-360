## The complaint

Entering a modification (or any relationship action) is painful: too much information at once.
The room dumps route question + package header + full facility list + figures simultaneously.

## The choreography (founder's words, structured)

1. FIRST: only the opening question. "What do you want to do" (modify / renew / new; the
   relationship room's equivalent) stands alone on a calm stage. Nothing else competing.
2. THEN: the product package blends in - identity first, the anchor the action runs against.
3. THEN: the facilities blend in.
4. AS EACH TIER ARRIVES, the tier above fades out. Earlier content is never lost - it is
   summonable ("you can show earlier anyway") - but it leaves the stage. The room always shows
   the tier the banker is deciding on, not the accumulation.
5. The exits must be SEXY: shimmer out / fade out in the Electric Glass motion language (the
   draw-in with glow, the odometer, the seed entrance are the existing vocabulary; the exit
   choreography joins that family). No hard cuts, no instant unmounts.
