Decisions locked

  • Aggregation-first, package-anchored, for every action: single loans, covenants, collateral are member selections inside the Product Package, never standalone anchors.
  • nCino's APIs do the domain surgery (credit actions, clones, junctions); our layer does governance: plan, hash, single-use token, approver identity check, verification by re-query. Nothing is re-implemented.
  • Every call runs under the user's own identity. No service account, no elevation.
  • Commercial framing stays. Early-warning trackers out of scope. Credit memo and spreading as call-outs. UI work aligned with Debra's standards.
  • Sandbox approval emails are acceptable during build (Fabian, Aug 20).
  • Nothing built on unverified ground: observe the wire before pinning a shape; receipts before any done claim.
