Credit 360 · The 10-Minute Demo

The point of view, in one breath: a commercial banker today is the human middleware between five systems: the loan system, the spreading tool, servicing, the inbox, and the spreadsheet that glues them together. Credit 360 deletes that job. The systems meet in one living view, the banker talks to the whole book in plain English, nothing is retyped, nothing is invented, and nothing becomes real without a human approving it.

The three sentences that carry the demo (say them verbatim)

  1. Aggregation: “Nobody assembled this page. It assembled itself, live, from the bank’s own systems, the moment I clicked.”
  2. The deal room: “I’m not operating software. I told the bank what the client needs, and the bank’s own paperwork happened in the bank’s own system, with my approval in the middle.”
  3. The full circle: “The request arrived by email, the work happened in the system of record, and the reply drafted itself. Nobody touched a form, and the audit trail wrote itself.”

Why this way: the aggregation story (the strong point, expanded)

Banks do not lack systems; they lack a place where the systems meet. The loan lives in nCino. The financials live in the spreading tool. The payment behaviour lives in servicing. The client lives in the inbox. Today a banker carries numbers between them by hand, and every carried number is an hour spent and an error waiting. Credit 360 is not another system: it is the meeting place. Every figure on the glass is a live read from the system that owns it, so the page cannot drift from the truth, and every change spoken in the room lands back in the system that owns it. One conversation, five systems, zero re-keying.

Q&A (two sentences each, no jargon)

Does it make mistakes?It only shows figures it read from the bank’s systems, and it refuses to invent what it cannot read. Every change is shown to a person before anything is filed.
What if it is wrong?Nothing becomes real without human approval, and everything filed is traceable to who approved it and when. Wrong stops at the review step.
Does it replace my people?It replaces the retyping, not the judgment. The banker spends the saved hours with clients; the credit decision stays with the committee.
Is our data safe?It reads through the bank’s own authenticated connections with the signed-in user’s permissions. Nothing leaves the bank’s systems; the view is assembled, not copied.
Why not just ChatGPT?A general chatbot can talk about banking; this is wired into the bank’s systems of record with governance on every write. The difference is the last mile: filing, correctly, with approval.
How long to set up?It runs on connections the bank’s systems already expose. An engagement scopes it to your landscape; the pattern you saw is the product.
What does it cost?Engagement-dependent; the honest frame is hours returned per banker per week, and you saw four minutes replace three weeks.
Could this work for [other area]?The pattern is general: systems meet in one view, people speak, the system of record files. Commercial credit is where we proved it first.
