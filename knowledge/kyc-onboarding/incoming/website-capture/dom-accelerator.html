How to start

Three decisions, then you're moving.

No rip-and-replace. Pick who feels the value first, the surface they live on, and a new capability to lead with.

Decision 01

Who feels the value first?

Start with the group where the pain is most visible and the win is most measurable. Advisors feel the productivity gain immediately. CSAs feel the process relief. Clients feel the speed. Pick one group, prove the value, then expand. Accenture has mapped 40 core CSA processes across FA, CSA, and client personas — we help you pick the starting point with the clearest path to a measurable win.

Image
Image

Decision 02

Where do they already live?

The engagement layer is the surface your users already work on — web, mobile, Slack, Teams, or Claude Desktop. The right answer isn't the most sophisticated one, it's the one with the lowest adoption barrier. If your advisors live in Teams, start there. We've built the custodian integrations and compliance requirements across every major engagement surface — the choice of channel doesn't change what's possible, just where you start.

Decision 03

Start with what doesn't exist yet.

Don't migrate existing workflows first. New capabilities — meeting prep, onboarding compression, compliance triage — have no legacy to compete with. They land as additions, not replacements. That's the lowest-risk, fastest-value entry point. Accenture's NeuraFlash and SFBG practice have taken this from concept to deployed solution — we know which first use cases build momentum and which ones stall.

First use cases

Three contained starting points.

Each is self-contained, measurable, and expandable — choose by the case you most need to make.

Option 01

Onboarding compression

The highest-ROI starting point. Every day between signed client and funded account is fee revenue on hold. This use case compresses onboarding from weeks to hours for low-risk cases — straight-through processing, automated KYC routing, and parallel document workflows. The metrics are immediate and easy to defend in any executive conversation.

20d → <24h · 25%+ → <5% NIGO

Option 02

Advisor meeting prep

The fastest path to advisor adoption. Every meeting arrives with a full AI-generated brief — portfolio summary, recent life events, outstanding tasks, next-best-action suggestions. No login required, no manual prep. Advisors feel the value on day one, which builds the internal momentum that makes everything else easier to roll out.

30% of workday returned · immediate day-one value

Option 03

Compliance queue triage

The strongest case for the CDO and compliance officer. Automated KYC/AML screening, proactive flag surfacing, and a full audit trail in Salesforce for every agent action. Lowest disruption to existing workflows — it runs alongside what's already there. The compliance team signs off first, which clears the path for everything that follows.

$10M–$100M+ fine exposure reduced · full audit trail
