Four value levers
Where the $606M comes from.
Each lever is modelled independently and measured against a real book. Together they compound.
Onboarding Compression
Funded accounts arrive faster. Revenue doesn't wait.
Every day between signed client and funded account is fee revenue on hold. At 500 new HNW accounts a year, compressing onboarding from 20 days to under 24 hours moves weeks of revenue per account from delayed to earned. That compounds fast.

Advisor Productivity
Same team. Larger book. No new headcount.
When 30% of the workday shifts from admin back to relationships, advisors don't just feel better — they carry more. More households per advisor means the same headcount manages a larger book. That's operating leverage without a hiring plan.

Compliance & Risk Reduction
Platform-enforced compliance. The fine that never happens.
FINRA and SEC fines aren't theoretical. They're public, they're large, and they follow firms that rely on individuals to enforce compliance manually. Platform-enforced compliance — every action logged, every check automated — removes that dependency entirely.

Contact Center Deflection
Fewer calls. Lower cost. Clients who never had to ask.
At $16 per inbound call, volume is a cost center. Proactive AI notifications answer the question before the client asks it. Fewer calls, lower cost, and clients who feel attended to without picking up the phone.
